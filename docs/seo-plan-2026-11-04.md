## 3. What is missing to become number one

Ranked by impact. Items 1 to 3 are the ones the leaders have and we do not.

1. **Authority.** The Oct 10 audit found 640 referring domains, 396 of them toxic (now disavowed), and the old domain's 744 links lost. The KD 30+ money terms ("porcelain veneers miami" 590/mo KD 34, "miami veneers" 590 KD 32) will not move on content alone. Target 30 to 50 real referring domains in six months from: the lender "find a provider" directories (Cherry, Sunbit, CareCredit, LendingClub, Alphaeon all link to enrolled practices); local press (Miami New Times, Miami Herald health, Brickell Magazine, El Nuevo Herald, Telemundo 51 segments); out-of-state press in Dallas, Houston and Atlanta on the "fly to Miami for veneers" angle; dental-school alumni pages for the four doctors; podcast guest spots; one local scholarship or sponsorship page per year. Budget decision needed.
2. **Local pack.** B1, B5 and B6 above, repeated monthly. Measure with the Position Tracking local-pack filter. Review velocity target: ten per month, replies within 48 hours, Spanish replies to Spanish reviews.
3. **Brand entity separation.** Google treats "xiluet" as the plastic-surgery group. A6 (schema), B6 (identical name everywhere) and a claimed Knowledge Panel for "Xiluet Smiles" fix this over two to three months. Do not chase the bare "xiluet" query; it was never dental traffic.
4. **One owning page per money term**, with anchors and links to match. The keyword map in section 4 is the rule; the Semrush Cannibalization tab is the monthly check.
5. **Topical depth.** The leaders rank 50+ pages on this keyword set; we rank 23. Keep two posts a week from the data: "are veneers permanent" (3,600 KD 1), "temporary veneers" (1,300 KD 0), "hollywood smile" (2,900 KD 24), veneers vs bonding, how to read a before-and-after photo; Spanish-first: "cuánto cuestan las carillas en Miami", "carillas en Hialeah", "diseño de sonrisa precio Miami". Skip topics already published ("what do teeth look like under veneers" is live).
6. **SERP features.** People Also Ask and AI Overview boxes appear on about 115 of the 124 tracked keywords and the site holds none. Every post already has an `answer` block and FAQ schema; the gap is phrasing. Pull the actual PAA questions per keyword from Semrush Keyword Overview and make the FAQ questions match them word for word.
7. **Video.** The video feature appears on about 35 tracked keywords. Put the consented patient-story clips on YouTube with titles such as "Porcelain veneers in Miami: [city] patient, 20 veneers in one week", description linking to the matching city page, and `VideoObject` schema on the pages that embed them.
8. **Page speed.** Mobile Lighthouse 37 on Oct 13 (LCP 10.7 s). Target 80+. Self-host the hero video, keep GTM deferred, check Core Web Vitals in Search Console monthly.
9. **Measurement cadence.** Every Monday: Position Tracking overview and the two Rankings tables into `seo/` (screenshot or export), Search Console 28-day export, and a five-line scorecard (top-10 count, visibility, non-brand clicks, form leads, reviews). Monthly: Semrush Organic Research export, Backlink Audit, Cannibalization tab.
10. **Semrush API units.** Without them the keyword gap, competitor and backlink reports stay manual. A full monthly refresh costs roughly 1,000 units for the organic pull, 200 for a keyword batch, plus competitor and backlink rows.
