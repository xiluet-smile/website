## 4. Q&A (seed these yourself from the owner account, then answer them)

Google lets the owner post questions and answers. Post the question first, then answer it from the business account. Keep answers under 500 characters so they show without truncation.

1. **How much do porcelain veneers cost at Xiluet Smiles?**
   A single veneer is $1,000. Twenty veneers are $5,999 as the Smile Design package, which includes the design preview, cleaning, X-rays and a 5-year written warranty. The price is confirmed in writing within 6 hours of sending photos.
2. **How long does it take to get veneers?**
   Three visits in one week. Our lab is in the same building, so design, preparation and delivery happen in as little as 4 business days. Most out-of-state patients fly in Monday and go home Thursday.
3. **Can I see the result before you start?**
   Yes. At the first visit we scan your teeth and photograph your face, design the new shape and place a preview in your mouth. You approve it, or we change it, before any tooth is prepared.
4. **Do veneers hurt?**
   The tooth is numbed for the 0.3 mm of preparation, and most patients describe pressure, not pain. Temporaries can feel sensitive to cold for a day or two; after bonding that settles within a few days.
5. **How much of my tooth is removed?**
   Usually about 0.3 mm, and on some small or inward-set teeth none at all. Your doctor confirms this tooth by tooth on your scan at the first visit.
6. **Do you offer financing?**
   Yes. Cherry, Sunbit, CareCredit, LendingClub and Alphaeon all pre-qualify with a soft credit check. Twenty veneers work out to about $174 a month on a 48-month plan at 9% APR.
7. **Do you speak Spanish? / ¿Hablan español?**
   Yes, every doctor and the front desk speak English and Spanish. Sí, todos los doctores y la recepción hablan inglés y español.
8. **Where do out-of-state patients stay and how do they get to the clinic?**
   You book your own flights and hotel; we send a short hotel list with partner rates together with your estimate. The clinic is about 15 minutes from Miami International Airport with free parking.
9. **Is there a warranty?**
   Every package carries a 5-year written warranty on the veneers, crowns and bridges we make.
10. **Can I get veneers on just my front teeth?**
   Yes. Two, four, six or eight upper front veneers at $1,000 each is common, matched by eye to the teeth next to them with you in the chair.
